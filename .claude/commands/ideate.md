Use the `startup-ideation` skill to help the user generate and evaluate startup or product ideas.

Start by asking:
1. Are you generating new ideas, or evaluating ideas you already have?
2. What problem space or market are you interested in?
3. What's your unfair advantage? (background, network, insights)

**If generating:** Use the frameworks from Paul Graham, Kevin Hale, and others — look for problems the user has personally experienced, ideas that seem embarrassingly small, and markets that are changing.

**If evaluating:** Apply the idea maze framework, evaluate founder-market fit, check for genuine insight vs. obvious ideas, and assess the market timing.
