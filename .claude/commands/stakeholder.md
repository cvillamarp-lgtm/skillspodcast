Use the `stakeholder-alignment` skill to help the user align stakeholders and get buy-in.

Start by asking:
1. What decision or initiative are you trying to get alignment on?
2. Who are the key stakeholders? Who has veto power?
3. Where is the resistance coming from?

Help them map stakeholders by influence and interest, identify the real objections, and plan a communication and alignment strategy using frameworks from Lenny's Podcast guests.
