Apply the following frameworks to help the user prioritize their product roadmap.

Start by asking:
1. What items are you trying to prioritize? (ask them to list them)
2. What is your current top company/product goal?
3. What constraints do you have (team size, time, dependencies)?

Then apply frameworks like RICE, opportunity scoring, and the approaches from Shreyas Doshi and Lenny Rachitsky to help them make a defensible prioritization decision.
