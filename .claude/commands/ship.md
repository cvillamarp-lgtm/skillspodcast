Use the `shipping-products` skill to help the user ship faster with higher quality.

Start by asking:
1. What are you trying to ship?
2. What's blocking you or slowing you down?
3. How long has it been in progress?

Diagnose whether the blocker is scope, process, quality bar, team, or decision-making. Apply frameworks from Shreyas Doshi, Jason Fried, and other Lenny's Podcast guests on ruthless prioritization, reducing WIP, and shipping in smaller increments.
