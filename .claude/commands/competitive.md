Use the `competitive-analysis` skill to help the user understand and respond to competition.

Start by asking:
1. Who are you analyzing? (specific competitor or general landscape?)
2. What decision is this analysis meant to inform?
3. What do you already know about them?

Help them build a structured competitive analysis: positioning, strengths/weaknesses, pricing, go-to-market, and strategic intent. Apply frameworks on finding differentiation and deciding when to compete head-on vs. find a different angle.
