Apply the following frameworks to help the user define their North Star metric.

Start by asking:
1. What does your product do, and who does it serve?
2. What does "value delivered to the customer" look like for you?
3. Do you have candidate metrics already?

Help them evaluate candidates against the criteria: does it measure value delivered to customers, does it predict long-term business success, can the team influence it? Apply frameworks from Lenny Rachitsky and Sean Ellis. Walk through the tradeoffs of different metric choices.
